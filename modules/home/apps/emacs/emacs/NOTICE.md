## Changes made relative to upstream Centaur Emacs v8.3.1

- **De-branded**: `centaur-*` symbols renamed to `flutter-*`; the
  Centaur logo (`logo.png`, `banner.txt`), the "Centaur Emacs" frame
  title, and the ASCII-art banner in `init.el` were removed; the
  dashboard title/footer now read "Flutter Emacs".
- **Re-themed**: the doom-themes/circadian/auto-dark theme machinery
  and the `centaur-theme(-alist/-auto/-system)` options were removed.
  The config loads the single `flutterice` theme
  (`themes/flutterice-theme.el`), generated by matugen from the
  desktop wallpaper (see `modules/home/services/matugen` in this
  flake). Theme switching is available via `M-x consult-theme` or the
  toggles hydra (`F6`).
- **Package manager**: Centaur's built-in package.el setup was replaced
  with Elpaca (`lisp/my-elpaca.el`, carried over from the previous
  old config). The package-archives mirror machinery is kept but
  inert; `M-x update-packages` now runs `elpaca-update-all`.
- **Extras layer**: `lisp/init-extras.el` carries over QML support
  (qml-ts-mode + qmlls, for editing this flake's `quickshell/` config),
  nix-mode/nil integration, and the empv/ement/elcord media stack,
  wired in Centaur's hydra idiom (`C-c m`).
- **Nix integration**: the config lives in this flake and is symlinked
  to `~/.config/emacs` (see `default.nix`); language servers,
  debuggers, and formatters come from Nix, not Elpaca.
